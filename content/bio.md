I am a **Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**. My work focuses on energy-flexible and grid-responsive buildings, demand response, renewable and storage optimization, and AI-enabled control for energy systems.

I obtained the B.Eng. degree from **Tsinghua University** in 2018 and the Ph.D. degree from **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University** in 2023, majoring in Building Environment and Energy Engineering. I have published **24 academic papers** in high-level journals such as Engineering, Applied Energy, Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews (16 as first/corresponding authors, 2 ESI highly cited papers, 1 ESI hot paper). My Google Scholar profile reports 936 citations. [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=nXW7KrwAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Hong-Tang-17)

I lead a PolyU start-up project and have contributed to projects funded by the Hong Kong Research Grants Council, China Southern Power Grid, Shenzhen Science and Technology Innovation Commission, and Sun Hung Kai Properties.
